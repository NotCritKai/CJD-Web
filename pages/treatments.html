CJD Treatments

There is currently no cure for CJD, and treatment primarily focuses on alleviating symptoms and providing supportive care. This may include medications to manage pain, muscle stiffness, and psychiatric symptoms, as well as physical therapy and palliative care to improve quality of life.

Treatments

There are no known treatments for CJD currently, however there are ways to make them more comfortable before death.

Communication

Early in the disease, they can draw up an advanced directive. This is used so they can communicate their decisions in case they become too ill in the later stages. This can decide the place of treatment, medicines, feeding tube, organ donation, and resuscitation.

Comfortable Places

Places of treatments include if they want to be cared for at home, in hospice, or in a hospital, with each person having a preference.

Support

They and their families each have a specialist team to offer clinical and emotional support.
